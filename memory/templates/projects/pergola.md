# Pallet pergola

Timber canopy in the alley outside the back door. Modelled on the millimetre bench.

- Site: alley about 1560 mm across, structure about 1 m along the alley, 2 m headroom at the midpoint. Garden wall ~2 m; house side is the two-storey extension.
- Stock (recovered pallet pine): 10× 1600×70×15, 3× 1300×90×20, 3× 1600×90×20. Not all must be used.
- Layout: three uprights at 333 mm centres; posts spliced where short; rafters cut to stay inside the alley.
- Bench snapshot: `pallet-pergola` under `~/.jarvis/bench/projects/`.
- Discuss design on the mouth. Place or change millimetres on the bench only when asked.
